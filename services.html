FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have another question? Call us or send a message — we're happy to help.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We work with most major insurance providers in the UAE and can process direct billing. Contact us with your provider details and we'll confirm your coverage before treatment.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Professional whitening performed by a dentist is completely safe. We assess your teeth and gums first and use clinically proven systems to minimise sensitivity.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacing after 15–20 years.

At what age should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth appearing.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Absolutely. We reserve same-day slots for emergencies such as severe pain, swelling or broken teeth. Call us immediately and we'll see you as soon as possible.
